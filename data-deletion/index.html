PATRIA ALFAJORES

Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: September 18, 2026

If you have communicated with PATRIA and would like us to delete personal information associated with your interactions with our business, you can submit a deletion request.

How to request deletion

  1. Send a direct message to PATRIA's official Instagram account, @alfajores.patria.
  2. State that you are requesting deletion of your personal data.
  3. Identify the Instagram account or other contact information associated with the records you want deleted.

We may ask for additional information when reasonably necessary to verify the request and locate the relevant records.

What happens next

After a valid request is verified, PATRIA will delete or anonymize the relevant personal information from systems under our control, except for information we need to retain to comply with applicable law, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, or maintain necessary business records.

Deletion from PATRIA's systems does not necessarily delete information held independently by Instagram, Meta, or other third-party platforms. Requests concerning data held by those platforms should be directed to the relevant platform.
