Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PATRIA Alfajores ("PATRIA", "we", "us") may collect, use, and store personal information when people communicate with our business, including through Instagram.

Information we may process

When you contact PATRIA through Instagram or another supported communication channel, we may process information you provide or that is made available through the platform, such as your name or username, platform-specific account identifier, message content, message metadata, contact details you choose to provide, and information related to your inquiry or order.

How we use information

We use this information to respond to inquiries, provide customer service, manage sales leads and follow-ups, process and coordinate orders, maintain customer records, and improve our customer-service processes.

Instagram integration

PATRIA uses Meta's Instagram API and webhooks to receive and manage communications sent to our Instagram business account. Information received through this integration is used for PATRIA's own customer-service and business-management purposes.

Storage and access

Relevant information may be stored in PATRIA's private customer relationship management systems and supporting service providers. Access is limited to people who need it to operate PATRIA and provide customer service.

Sharing and sale of data

We do not sell personal information. We may use service providers that process information on our behalf to operate our technical systems, subject to their applicable terms and privacy protections. We may also disclose information where required by law.

Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, including customer service, business records, and applicable legal obligations.

Your choices and deletion requests

You may ask PATRIA to delete personal information associated with your interactions with us. See our Data Deletion Instructions.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as our services or legal obligations change. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact PATRIA through our official Instagram account, @alfajores.patria. We may ask for enough information to identify the relevant account or records before acting on a request.
